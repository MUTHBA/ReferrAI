ReferrAI – Business Requirements Document (BRD)
1. Executive Summary

ReferrAI is a standalone referral and commission management platform that enables businesses to manage referral programs, automate commission calculations, track partner performance, detect fraudulent activity, and leverage AI-driven insights for decision-making.

The platform is configurable, allowing administrators to define commission structures, partner tiers, payout settings, and referral program rules without requiring code changes.

2. Business Objectives
Primary Objectives
Build a configurable referral and commission management platform.
Automate referral tracking and commission calculations.
Enable partner onboarding and performance monitoring.
Detect fraudulent referral activity.
Predict partner inactivity and churn risks.
Rank and identify high-performing partners using AI.
Provide explainable AI-driven insights.
Secondary Objectives
Reduce manual administrative effort.
Improve transparency for partners.
Provide actionable analytics and reporting.
3. Problem Statement

Businesses managing referral programs often face challenges such as:

Manual commission calculations.
Limited visibility into referral performance.
Undetected fraudulent referrals.
Inflexible commission structures.
Lack of insights into partner engagement and retention.

ReferrAI addresses these challenges through automation, configurable business rules, analytics, and AI-powered intelligence.

6. Business Requirements
Referral Management

The platform shall support referral links, referral codes, QR-code referrals, click tracking, conversion tracking, and configurable attribution models.

Commission Management

The platform shall support percentage-based, fixed-value, and tier-based commissions with automatic calculation on successful conversions.

Partner Tier Management

The platform shall support configurable tier structures and automatic tier upgrades based on performance thresholds.

Withdrawal Management

The platform shall maintain wallet balances, process withdrawal requests, and support configurable approval thresholds.

7. Administrative Configuration

Administrators shall be able to configure:

Commission percentages
Fixed commission amounts
Tier qualification thresholds
Minimum withdrawal amounts
Auto-approval thresholds
Referral eligibility windows

All configuration changes shall be logged and timestamped.

8.2 Referral Quality Scoring

8.3 Fraud Detection

Identify suspicious referral activities and generate fraud risk scores.

8.4 Partner Churn Prediction

Predict partners at risk of becoming inactive.

8.5 Smart Partner Ranking

Rank partners based on performance and business impact.

8.6 AI Partner Discovery

Recommend potential referral partners based on business compatibility.

8.7 Explainable AI

Provide clear explanations for AI-generated predictions and recommendations.

9. Success Criteria
Referral programs can be configured without code changes.
Commission calculations are automated and accurate.
AI-generated insights are available for referrals and partners.
Fraudulent activities can be identified and reviewed.
Partner performance and engagement can be monitored effectively.
The platform operates end-to-end from referral creation to commission tracking.
10. Competitive Advantage

ReferrAI differentiates itself by combining referral management with AI-powered referral scoring, fraud detection, churn prediction, partner discovery, and explainable AI capabilities within a single platform.
